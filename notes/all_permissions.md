# Permissions

This document will have a list of all the permissions currently in the app, as well as
what they do, and what category they fall under. This document will be updated as needed

The wildcard (\*) permission is granted to team admins by default, and will allow them to do anything without additional permissions.

### Team Permissions:

-   editTeam: Able to edit team details such as team name and description
-   deleteTeam: Able to delete the team
-   manageMembers: Able to manage team members, changing roles and permissions
-   manageProjects: Able to manage team projects
